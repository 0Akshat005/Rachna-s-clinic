Before you visit

Questions,
answered.

The information below is general; confirm clinic-specific policies before publication.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move. If you have relevant reports or previous prescriptions, the clinic can advise whether to bring them.

What happens during my first appointment?

Your physiotherapist may ask about your symptoms and goals, assess your movement and discuss suitable next steps with you.

How long does a session take?

Session length can depend on the type of appointment. Contact the clinic to confirm the expected duration.

Can I book by phone or WhatsApp?

You can contact the clinic through the call and WhatsApp options shown on this prototype. An appointment is confirmed only when the clinic responds.
